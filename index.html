Famous Dive Sites
North Ari Atoll

Best time: January–April (dry season) · Diving possible year-round

Maaya Thila

An iconic pinnacle dive site with intricate caves, dramatic drop-offs, and thrilling swim-throughs. One of the finest dive sites in the Maldives, teeming with marine life day and night.

Fish Head (Mushimasmigili)

A UNESCO-recognised marine protected area famed for resident grey reef sharks and colossal schools of blueline snappers. An unforgettable drift dive experience.

Hafsa Thila

A classic thila offering encounters with white-tip reef sharks, napoleon wrasse, and a stunning variety of reef fish in crystal-clear blue water.

Located at the harbour corner of Ukulhas, this thrilling site offers encounters with multiple shark species — a must for shark enthusiasts.

Shark Dive

Rasdhoo Madivaru

Famous for its resident population of grey reef sharks and exciting channel diving. Nutrient-rich currents attract spectacular pelagic marine life.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your dive adventure begins.

Do I need any experience to try scuba diving?

No experience is needed for our Discover Scuba Diving program! After a short briefing and shallow-water practice session with an instructor, you'll head straight to the reef. If you can swim, you can dive.

How long does the PADI Open Water course take?

The full PADI Open Water Diver course typically takes 3–4 days, covering theory, confined water skills, and 4 open water training dives. You can accelerate the process by completing PADI eLearning theory before you arrive.

What is the best time of year to dive in North Ari Atoll?

The dry season (January–April) offers the best visibility — up to 30 m+ — and calmest seas. Diving is excellent year-round though. Whale sharks are resident in North Ari Atoll all year, while manta rays are most frequent from May to November.

Is scuba diving safe? What safety measures do you have in place?

Safety is our top priority. All instructors hold current PADI certifications, first aid, and rescue qualifications. We use well-maintained, regularly serviced equipment, keep groups small (max 4 divers per instructor), carry oxygen and a first aid kit on every boat, and conduct thorough pre-dive briefings.

Do you provide all the diving equipment?

Yes! We provide all necessary gear including wetsuits, masks, fins, BCDs, regulators, and tanks. All equipment is regularly serviced and thoroughly sanitised between uses. You're equally welcome to bring your own gear.

Can children participate in diving activities?

Children aged 8+ can enjoy PADI Bubblemaker (up to 2 m in shallow water). From age 10, they can enrol in PADI Junior Open Water. All junior divers must be accompanied by a certified adult and dive within age-appropriate depth limits.

How do I get to Ukulhas Island?

Ukulhas is reachable from Malé via a domestic flight to Rasdhoo (≈30 min) plus a short speedboat transfer, or by a direct speedboat from Malé (≈2.5–3 hours). We can arrange transfers upon request — just message us on WhatsApp.

How do I book, and what is the cancellation policy?

Book easily via WhatsApp, email, or the contact form below. A deposit secures your booking. Cancellations 48 hours or more in advance receive a full refund; within 48 hours the deposit is non-refundable. Contact us for group packages and special enquiries.

Are there medical requirements for diving?

Most healthy adults can dive. You'll complete a standard PADI medical questionnaire before any water activities. Certain conditions (recent surgery, heart conditions, severe asthma, etc.) may require a medical clearance from your doctor. Contact us if you have any health concerns before booking.
